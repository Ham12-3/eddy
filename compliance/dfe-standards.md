# DfE standards for an AI tutor: compliance matrix

Status: **DRAFT, 12 November 2026, for review by the product owner.** Drafted by the build team from the repository on branch `m10-hardening` (last commit 3642fa8, M9). The DfE does not approve, certify or endorse products against these standards, and nothing here says it has. This is Eddy's own self-assessment, not reviewed by anyone outside the team.

Sources:

- **Generative AI: product safety standards** (GOV.UK collection "Generative AI: product safety expectations", published 22 January 2025, last updated 19 January 2026, which added cognitive development, emotional and social development, mental health and manipulation). The document has 13 headings; the rows below follow them. The headings and the one-line paraphrases of each expectation were taken from an automated read of the GOV.UK page on 12 November 2026: **check them against the primary document before relying on this matrix.** The M0 skeleton's rows have been adjusted to match (adding "Educational use cases").
- **Meeting digital and technology standards in schools and colleges** (GOV.UK, last updated 16 September 2026). These standards are written for schools and colleges, not suppliers; the second table records how Eddy supports a school meeting the ones relevant to an AI tutor.

"Evidence" cites only what is committed: code, tests, evals and the PLAN.md evidence log, with paths a reviewer can open. Nothing has been used by a real student yet. Status is **Met**, **Partly met**, **Not met** or **Not applicable**; gaps are listed under each table.

Work in progress in the working tree on 12 November 2026 (uncommitted and not yet checked, so not counted here): the worker running the alerts, purge and erasure jobs every 15 minutes (`services/api/src/eddy_api/worker.py`), `pnpm audit` and `pip-audit` steps in CI (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`), an admin cost view over `llm_call` (`services/api/src/eddy_api/routes/admin.py`), and parents' own data rights.

### Gaps

- **Stated purpose.** The purpose is in SPEC.md, an internal document; nothing yet publishes it to students, parents or schools. No evidence of impact on learning exists; claims must wait for pilot data.
- **Educational use cases.** No pilot or study with real students. The learner model's parameters are starting points to refit on pilot data (ADR-0026).
- **Filtering.** The tripwire list and safeguarding script are drafts that Eddy's safeguarding lead must approve (`SCRIPT_APPROVED = False`; until then the tutor starts only in development and test, `services/api/src/eddy_api/routes/tutor.py`). Filtering is English only and is not varied by age or SEND need (one 13+ band). No documented process to update the tripwires as new harms emerge. The prompt-injection red-team pass is M10 work, not done.
- **Monitoring and reporting.** No school or local supervisor exists in the B2C MVP, so no school-facing reports, no reports on access attempts for non-expert staff, and nothing on cognitive offloading or engagement for teachers (no teacher view, SPEC.md 2.3). No safeguarding lead is named, so `SAFEGUARDING_ALERT_EMAIL` has no verified owner and no agreed response time. The alerts job runs only by command (scheduling is uncommitted M10 work). There is no admin safeguarding queue (deferred, ADR-0032); events are reviewed only in the database.
- **Security.** No OWASP ASVS Level 2 checklist, automated security scan or k6 load test yet (PLAN.md M10, all open). The strict nonce CSP is not built (ADR-0032). No dependency scanning in committed CI. Admin pages run only in development or with `EDDY_ADMIN_PREVIEW=1` and have no sign-in or permission levels. Nothing is deployed, so secrets management (AWS Secrets Manager), patching and monitoring in production do not exist yet (ADR-0014). The per-lesson lock is not tested under real concurrency (PLAN.md M7).
- **Privacy and data protection.** The DPIA is not complete or signed off; no legal review; zero data retention is not confirmed with Anthropic; no data processing agreements or UK transfer mechanisms are in place; the controller and processors are not named in the notices; retention for safety records and the audit log is not set.
- **Intellectual property.** The no-training commitment from Anthropic is not yet confirmed in writing; the ZDR gate keeps real student data from Claude until it is. Copyright advice is a proposal until a legal reviewer signs it off.
- **Design and testing.** No testing with real or diverse users. The LLM eval suites are not run automatically in CI (opt-in, they cost money). No production monitoring that the product keeps performing as intended.
- **Governance.** No formal risk assessment is signed off (the DPIA is in draft). No formal complaints process or response time for Report a concern. No published policy on AI safety decisions. Most roles in governance.md are not yet named.
- **Cognitive development.** No record of engagement with educators, child-safety experts or psychologists; no child-safety training for the team; no published child development impact plan. Offloading is recorded per attempt (`aided`) but not reported to anyone, and there is no teacher view.
- **Emotional and social development.** The product has a name, Eddy, and the tutor and the safeguarding script speak in the first person ("I am an AI"). The standard asks to avoid names and "I" statements that imply personhood; the product owner should decide whether a named, first-person tutor that always states it is an AI is acceptable, and record why. The break note is a prompt only: there is no hard usage limit and no teacher override. Repeated disclosures or worrying patterns are not detected across lessons; each event is reviewed on its own.
- **Mental health.** The response is a single script for every category and severity, not a tiered pathway (for example softer signposting for MEDIUM distress, CEOP for exploitation); this is an open question for the safeguarding lead. No child mental health expertise has reviewed the design; no published crisis protocol; no time-of-day or isolation-pattern detection.

### Gaps

- **Filtering and monitoring.** No integration with a school's DSL or monitoring system; no school pilot yet (SPEC.md 16, question 5).
- **Safeguarding.** No named safeguarding lead; script and tripwires unapproved; no admin review queue; alerting not scheduled; what can be shared with emergency services is undecided.
- **Data protection.** No school-facing processor terms; see section 1.
- **Cyber security.** No ASVS checklist, scan, backup plan or tested restore; no production secrets management; the runbooks are untested drafts.
- **Cloud solutions.** Not deployed; no assessment of the AWS set-up, resilience or backups. Deploying needs the product owner's approval (ADR-0014).
- **Digital accessibility.** No keyboard-only journey through the core loop yet (PLAN.md M8, open). The NVDA and VoiceOver checklist has not been run. No accessibility statement is published. A scene and a due review are not yet driven end to end in E2E.
