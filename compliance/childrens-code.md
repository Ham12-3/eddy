# ICO Age Appropriate Design Code: checklist

Status: **DRAFT, 12 November 2026, for review by the product owner.** Drafted by the build team from the repository on branch `m10-hardening`; it describes the code at commit 1f5a9c4 (M10). It is not legal advice and has had no legal review (SPEC.md section 16, question 2). Every standard applies to every Eddy account, whatever the age (ADR-0002).

How to read this: "Planned approach" summarises SPEC.md. "Evidence" cites only what is committed in the repository: code, tests, E2E specs and the PLAN.md evidence log, with paths a reviewer can open. Nothing has been used by a real student yet. Status is **Met**, **Partly met**, **Not met** or **Not applicable**; every gap is listed under the table.

## Remaining gaps, by standard

1. **Best interests.** No documented best-interests assessment, and no consultation with children or parents yet (DPIA section 3 lists students, parents and a school pilot as planned). The safeguarding script and tripwires are unapproved drafts and no safeguarding lead is named (`compliance/governance.md`). The learner model has not met real students (ADR-0026).
2. **DPIA.** Not complete, not signed off, no legal review. Open inside it: the lawful basis for 13 to 15 year olds (legitimate interests proposed), retention of `safeguarding_event`, `concern_report` and `audit_log` (ADR-0024; PLAN.md M8 deliverable still open), and processor contracts.
3. **Age-appropriate application.** Age assurance is self-declaration only. That is a deliberate choice, made proportionate by giving every account the same high-privacy defaults, but the DPIA must record the reasoning and the residual risk of under-13s lying about their age.
4. **Transparency.** Both notices are drafts awaiting legal review; the controller, the privacy contact, and the hosting and email processors are not yet named in them (`privacy.tsx`, "Who we are" and "Who else handles it"). The reading age of the short notice has not been measured. The notice says data reaches Anthropic only once zero data retention is confirmed; that confirmation has not happened (SPEC.md 16, question 4). No just-in-time explanation exists at the moment a message is sent to the tutor, beyond the notice.
6. **Policies and community standards.** The tutor's behaviour rules are not published to students or parents in plain words, and there are no published terms of use. There is no documented process, response time or owner for handling concern reports, and no admin queue to read them (ADR-0032 deferred it); today they can be read only in the database.
8. **Data minimisation.** Retention for safeguarding events, concern reports and the audit log is not set. Free text a student types to the tutor can contain personal details and is sent to Claude as written; redaction before Claude is deferred (PLAN.md M7, "Recorded, not fixed"). The jobs are scheduled in the worker, but nothing is deployed, so they run only where the worker runs (ADR-0014, ADR-0034). Access logs may hold addresses in sign-in link URLs (PLAN.md M8, to strip in M10).
9. **Data sharing.** No data processing agreement or UK transfer mechanism (IDTA or UK Addendum) is in place with Anthropic, and zero data retention is not confirmed. Hosting (AWS) and email (Amazon SES) are planned, not contracted or deployed (ADR-0014). What Eddy may share with emergency services or children's social care after a safeguarding event is undecided (runbooks/safeguarding.md).
12. **Profiling.** The DPIA has not yet set out the justification for profiling as part of the core service, the balancing test, or why it cannot be switched off. The learner model's parameters are starting points to refit on pilot data, and a student who never learns a node but guesses right 1 time in 5 is falsely marked SECURE about a fifth of the time (ADR-0026, "Consequences"); the DPIA should weigh that.
15. **Online tools.** Report a concern needs the student to be signed in; there is no route for someone signed out or for a parent beyond the email notices. No one is named to answer concern reports, and there is no admin queue or response time. Requests the notice lists other than download and delete (correction, restriction, objection) go through Report a concern and are handled by hand ([runbooks/data-rights.md](runbooks/data-rights.md), a draft). Parents have no Report a concern route of their own.
